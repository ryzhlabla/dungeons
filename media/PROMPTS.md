# Иллюстрации прототипа

Созданы встроенным image_gen (навык imagegen), не через API/CLI. Ниже исходные промпты. JPG в scenes — рабочие копии; заменяйте их своими изображениями.

## road_end

Use case: illustration-story. Create a landscape 16:9 image for a Telegram adventure game scene. End of a narrow forest road, a small old stone cottage with a wooden door and a softly warm glowing window, a stream running alongside into deep green forest. Atmospheric illustrated adventure book, painterly cinematic environment art, stone grey, deep green, muted blue, warm amber accents. Twilight but legible on a small phone. Inviting mystery, not horror. No people, no text, no borders, no UI. Single full-frame scene.
